# Curriculum: A2

Status: **approved 2026-10-26**.

Planned A2 lessons, in teaching order. Each lesson follows `SPEC-content-format.md`: one short original text in American English, one grammar point explained in pt-BR, a glossary and auto-graded exercises.

The themes follow her goals: travel, TV series, short articles, and daily life. B1 and B2 will be outlined later, from what she finds easy or hard at A2.

## Unit a2-u1: O dia a dia (review)

The basics she already partly knows, revisited through reading so that the first lessons feel easy.

| # | Lesson id | Grammar point | Text | pt-BR title |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `a2-present-simple-routine` | Present simple; adverbs of frequency (always, usually, never) | A day in the life of a barista in New York | Um dia na vida de uma barista |
| 2 | `a2-present-continuous-scene` | Present continuous; present simple vs continuous | What is happening in a scene of a sitcom | O que está acontecendo na cena? |
| 3 | `a2-there-is-are-neighborhood` | There is / there are; some / any | A hotel's description of its neighborhood | O bairro do hotel |

## Notes

- **Order:** unit 1 is a review, to build confidence with reading before the new grammar. If it feels too easy, she can skip ahead; nothing in the format forces the order.
- **Lesson ids:** they are final once a lesson is written, because progress will be stored by lesson id.
- **The spec's example lesson** (the London weekend) is replaced by lesson 4, set in Chicago, to keep the texts American.
